# Code of conduct

Study Planner contributors should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing code or product decisions.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Accept correction, acknowledge impact, and repair harm where possible.
- Protect private information. This project can involve sensitive educational data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Do not disclose another person's private information in an issue. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban a contributor when necessary to protect the community. They should handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate an outcome to the reporter when it is safe to do so.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
